\section{Limitations and future work}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{A truthful writer.} The audit checks that recorded decisions were the prescribed
ones; it cannot check that the record is true. A component that misbehaves and records
consistently passes. Evidence independence (\cref{sec:eval-compliance}) narrows what an
accidental defect can hide; a deliberate one needs attestation of the code that writes the
ledger --- measured boot of the appliance and, per substrate, attestation evidence recorded with
each placement.

\paragraph{The classifier is the trusted base.} \Cref{prop:leak} moves the whole risk into
two numbers: how often material is stored where the policy does not expect it, and how often
the judge misses. Our E2 judge has a known miss rate by construction; the next measurement is a
real one --- a calibrated model with its threshold fitted on labelled documents --- including
under adaptive inputs written to look harmless. The independence of misfiling and misjudging is
an assumption to test, not a fact.

\paragraph{Declassification.} Briefs and redaction let material out by design. We reclassify
their output and seal matters that must never travel, but do not prove that a brief of
restricted material carries only what its class permits; permissive information-flow
analysis~\citep{siddiqui2024permissive} is the natural route.

\paragraph{Tools that do not declare their egress.} The network ceiling covers tools the policy
knows reach the network. A tool whose code sends data through a channel it does not declare
is out of our threat model; sandboxing the tool is the complement.

\paragraph{The adversary in E2--E4 is scripted.} It always attempts the most damaging sequence,
which bounds what a model could do with those tools, but it does not adapt. E5 uses a real
model under AgentDojo's attacks; adaptive attacks against the classifier remain to be run.

\section{Conclusion}

Where an agent's step runs is a security decision taken thousands of times a day. \annona{}
takes it per step, under a default-deny policy over a class that only rises, holds rather than
degrades, treats network tools as the substrates they are, and records every decision so that
someone holding only the ledger and the policy can check it. Measured with canaries no policy
lists, the perimeter leaks exactly what its classifier misses; with a calibrated judge that
becomes a number an operator chooses. The system, the harness and every result are open.
